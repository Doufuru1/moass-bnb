What is WSB?

WSB is a crypto-native meme inspired by the legendary short-squeeze culture of the GameStop era.

Born from the spirit of Apes, Diamond Hands, and community conviction, WSB brings that iconic meme culture into the crypto world.

Not about complicated mechanisms or promises of financial returns. It is about community, memes, culture, and the belief that the biggest squeeze is always one more move away.

WSB is an independent crypto meme project and is not affiliated with GameStop, WallStreetBets, or any other company.
